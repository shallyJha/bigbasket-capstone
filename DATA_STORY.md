## Recommendations

1. Prioritize Fruits & Vegetables for immediate action.
   - It has the largest percentage shortfall on the dashboard at 18.4% below target, worse than Snacks & Beverages at 16.2%.
   - Closing this gap needs the biggest revenue lift among all categories, so a targeted promotion or restock push here has the most room to move the needle.
2. Protect Dairy & Eggs from slipping into the Critical tier.
   - At 14.6% below target it sits just inside the 15% watch threshold, only about 65 in revenue away from crossing into Critical alongside Snacks & Beverages and Fruits & Vegetables.
   - A small, low cost promotion aimed at closing its 2,410 gap would keep it from joining the two worst performing categories.
